# Research Paper Writing Rules

General rules for every research paper, collected from my teacher's feedback.
Check each paper against this list before showing it to anyone.

---

## 1. Writing style
1. **Never use "we", "I", "our", "ours" or "us"** anywhere in the paper: abstract, body, captions, tables, footnotes.
   Use third-person or passive academic style: "This study proposes…", "The method is evaluated…", "The results show…".
2. **The paper must not look AI-generated, not even a little.**
   - No generic filler phrases ("In today's rapidly evolving world…", "delve into", "it is worth noting that", "plays a pivotal role", "a testament to", "in conclusion, this paper has…").
   - No repetitive sentence patterns, no lists of three adjectives, no over-polished marketing tone.
   - Every claim must be specific: give concrete numbers, datasets and comparisons instead of vague praise.
   - Read every paragraph aloud and rewrite anything that sounds like a template.
3. Keep terminology consistent: the same term for the same thing throughout the paper.

## 2. Abstract
4. **One single paragraph that covers four parts, in this order:**
   1. Background / context of the topic,
   2. The problem or limitation in existing methods,
   3. The proposed method (what it is and how it works),
   4. The results: how much it improves on or outperforms existing algorithms, with numbers.

## 3. Title and journal
5. **The title, template and footer must match the target journal** (e.g. Neurocomputing vs Expert Systems with Applications). Decide the journal first and follow its style exactly.

## 4. Proposed methodology
6. **Start the methodology section with an introductory paragraph** before the first subsection.
7. **Give all equations of the proposed method** (numbered: Eq. 1, Eq. 2, …), not only pseudocode.
8. **Explain every symbol** used in each equation.
9. **Explain the impact of each equation on the results**, in paragraph form.
10. **Include one complete methodology diagram (graphical abstract)** that shows the whole pipeline from input to output.

## 5. Section length
11. **No section may be short or summary-like.** Introduction, Related Work, Baseline, Methodology, Results, Discussion and Conclusion must all be detailed.
12. **The Discussion must be 1 to 1.5 pages.**

## 6. References
13. **40–45 references** for a full journal paper.
14. **Every reference must be real and verified** against the publisher: authors, year, venue, volume, pages, DOI.
15. Do not depend on unpublished work that reviewers cannot access (e.g. "Part I of this study"). Make it citable or self-contained.

## 7. Results and integrity
16. **Only real results.** Never fabricate, edit, estimate or extrapolate numbers, and never present a subset as the full experiment. Fabrication can lead to retraction and harm the university's reputation.
17. Always state **which model, how many samples, what hardware** each result comes from.
18. If an experiment is smaller than planned, say so honestly in the caption and in the limitations.
19. Compare against methods whose code is available and that were run under the same conditions, and **justify the choice of baselines**. Numbers copied from other papers must be clearly labelled as such.

## 8. AI-free paper
20. **Write the paper fully by hand, in the authors' own words.** The text must read as human writing from start to finish (see rule 2).
21. **Do not add any AI statement or mention in the paper:** not in the methodology, not in the acknowledgements, not at the end (no "Declaration of generative AI…" section).
22. Any questions about journal policy are handled with the editor after submission, as the supervisor advises.

## 9. Final check before submission
- [ ] No "we / I / our / ours / us"
- [ ] Does not read as AI-generated
- [ ] Abstract: one paragraph, four parts, with numbers
- [ ] Title and template match the journal
- [ ] Methodology: intro paragraph, equations, symbols explained, impact explained, full diagram
- [ ] All sections detailed; Discussion 1–1.5 pages
- [ ] 40–45 verified references
- [ ] Only real results, with model, samples and hardware stated
- [ ] Written fully by hand; no AI statement or mention anywhere in the paper
